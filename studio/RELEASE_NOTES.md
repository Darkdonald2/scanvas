Scanvas Studio turns raw scans from the Scanvas iPhone app into dense, photo-textured models on your computer.

**Mac (Apple silicon):** download `ScanvasStudio-mac-apple-silicon.zip`, unzip, move *Scanvas Studio* to Applications. The first time, right-click it and choose **Open** (it isn't from the App Store).
**Windows:** download `ScanvasStudio-windows.zip`, unzip, open the folder and run **Scanvas Studio.exe**. Allow it on private networks when Windows asks, so the iPhone can send scans.
**Intel Mac or anything else:** download `ScanvasStudio-source.zip` and double-click the launcher for your system (needs Python 3.12 from python.org).

On the iPhone: Settings › Computer processing › Raw capture on, scan, then open the scan › ⋯ › Send to computer.
